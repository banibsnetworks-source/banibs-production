# Amendment A-0002: Emergency Urgency Defense

**Status**: RATIFIED  
**Date**: 2026-02-10  
**Axioms**: Axiom 2 (Time Sovereignty), Axiom 5 (Calm Technology)  
**Linked Test**: ST-002 (Emergency Urgency Attack)

---

## Problem Statement

Extractive platforms manipulate users through artificial urgency:
- Fake countdown timers that reset
- False scarcity ("Only 2 left!")
- Loss aversion triggers ("Don't miss out!")
- Notification bombardment
- Guilt-based retention ("We'll miss you!")

These tactics exploit psychological vulnerabilities to override rational decision-making, treating human attention as a resource to extract rather than respect.

---

## Patch Rule

### P1: No Artificial Urgency
The platform SHALL NOT use:
- Countdown timers unless tied to real deadlines
- Scarcity indicators unless inventory is genuinely limited
- "Last chance" language for recurring opportunities
- Animated urgency indicators (flashing, pulsing)

### P2: Notification Sovereignty
Users control their attention:
- All notification types individually toggleable
- "Quiet hours" respected absolutely
- No notification shall guilt or shame
- Unsubscribe is one click, no dark patterns

### P3: Calm Exit Paths
When users disengage:
- No guilt language ("We'll miss you")
- No fake "Are you sure?" barriers
- No hidden cancel buttons
- No retention dark patterns

### P4: Transparency on Time
If something is time-sensitive:
- State the real deadline clearly
- Explain why the deadline exists
- Do not manufacture false urgency

---

## Pass/Fail Criteria

### PASS
- No fake countdown timers anywhere in UI
- Notification preferences fully respected
- Unsubscribe/cancel is single action
- No guilt or shame language
- Real deadlines clearly explained

### FAIL
- Timers that reset or are fake
- Notifications ignore user settings
- Multiple steps to unsubscribe
- "We'll miss you" or similar guilt
- Urgency language for non-urgent things

---

## Implementation Notes

- Audit all UI copy for urgency language
- Review notification system permissions
- Test unsubscribe flows for friction
- Remove all FOMO-based marketing

---

*Respect for human time is non-negotiable. Urgency must be earned, not manufactured.*
