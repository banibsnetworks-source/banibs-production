# Amendment A-0007: Responsibility Granularity Framework

**Status**: RATIFIED  
**Date**: 2026-02-13  
**Axioms**: Axiom 1 (Human Agency First), Axiom 4 (Power Balance)  
**Linked Tests**: ST-003 (Infinite Analysis Loop), ST-005 (Moral Simplicity Allowance)

---

## Problem Statement

A-0005 lacks clarity on partial or distributed responsibility. This creates:

- All-or-nothing stalemates
- Refusal to acknowledge due to fear of total blame
- Inability to resolve multi-party situations
- Unfair burden on those willing to acknowledge partial role

The system requires a framework for proportional responsibility acknowledgment.

---

## Amendment Rule

### Responsibility Levels

Responsibility acknowledgment may be:

#### Full Responsibility (Primary Cause)
- Direct causal action
- Primary decision-maker
- Sole or dominant contributor

#### Partial Responsibility (Shared Contribution)
- Contributing factor among multiple causes
- Shared decision-making
- One of several actors

#### Contextual Responsibility (Indirect Influence)
- Environmental or systemic contribution
- Enabling conditions without direct action
- Influence without control

---

## Acknowledgment Requirement

HDOS requires:

**Explicit identification of one's proportional contribution, not total fault admission.**

### Valid Acknowledgments
- "I contributed to this outcome by [specific action]"
- "My role was [partial/contextual] because [reason]"
- "I acknowledge [specific contribution] while noting [other factors]"

### Invalid Evasions
- "I had nothing to do with it" (when evidence shows contribution)
- "It was entirely someone else's fault" (when evidence shows shared cause)
- Silence on own contribution while analyzing others

---

## Responsibility Evasion Trigger

Refusal to acknowledge **any** contribution when evidence supports partial involvement triggers Responsibility Evasion State.

### Trigger Conditions
- Evidence shows involvement at any level
- Direct question about specific contribution asked
- Acknowledgment of proportional role absent
- Pattern of deflection to other actors only

### Non-Triggers
- Genuine dispute over factual involvement
- Acknowledgment of contextual role when evidence supports only that
- Request for clarification on specific contribution expected

---

## Patch Rule

### P1: Granularity Option
System SHALL offer granular acknowledgment options, not binary admit/deny.

### P2: Proportional Focus
Acknowledgment requests SHALL specify the level being asked about.

### P3: Partial Acceptance
Partial acknowledgment SHALL satisfy requirement at that level.

### P4: Evidence Matching
Expected acknowledgment level SHALL match evidence strength.

---

## Rationale

This amendment:
- **Prevents stalemate over all-or-nothing admissions**
- **Allows proportional accountability**
- **Encourages partial acknowledgment over total denial**
- **Matches responsibility to evidence**
- **Enables resolution in multi-party situations**

---

## Pass/Fail Criteria

### PASS
- Granular acknowledgment options available
- Partial acknowledgment accepted as valid
- Evidence level matches expected acknowledgment
- Multi-party situations resolvable
- No all-or-nothing forced admissions

### FAIL
- Only binary admit/deny options
- Partial acknowledgment rejected
- Full admission demanded regardless of evidence
- Multi-party situations unresolvable
- Proportional responsibility impossible

---

*Responsibility is proportional. Acknowledgment should match contribution.*
