# Amendment A-0006: Harm Confirmation Standard

**Status**: RATIFIED  
**Date**: 2026-02-13  
**Axioms**: Axiom 3 (Transparency Mandate), Axiom 4 (Power Balance)  
**Linked Tests**: ST-001 (Abuse Shield Attack), ST-010 (Meta-Weaponization Loop Guard)

---

## Problem Statement

A-0005 assumes confirmed harm but does not define the confirmation threshold. Without a clear standard:

- False harm claims may be weaponized
- Genuine disputes remain unresolvable
- Responsibility Evasion State may trigger prematurely
- Accountability becomes arbitrary

The system requires an objective standard for when harm is "confirmed" versus "disputed."

---

## Amendment Rule

### Harm Confirmation Threshold

Harm is considered **"confirmed"** under HDOS when at least one of the following is met:

#### a) Objective Violation
Violation of agreed rule, boundary, or law that is:
- Documented or publicly stated
- Applicable to the context
- Verifiably breached

#### b) Verifiable Behavioral Action
Behavioral action causing measurable consequence that is:
- Observable by third party
- Documentable with evidence
- Causally linked to outcome

#### c) Mutual Acknowledgment
Pattern of behavior acknowledged by both parties:
- Explicit agreement that action occurred
- No dispute over factual occurrence
- Disagreement limited to interpretation or severity

#### d) Independent Validation
Third-party validation in disputed contexts:
- Neutral party with no stake
- Access to relevant evidence
- Documented finding

---

## Harm Dispute State

If none of the above confirmation criteria are satisfied, system enters **Harm Dispute State**:

1. **Suspension**: Accountability enforcement is suspended
2. **Clarification**: Focus shifts to evidence gathering and definition alignment
3. **Gate**: No Responsibility Evasion State may be triggered until harm is confirmed

### Harm Dispute State Activities
- Request specific evidence
- Clarify definitions and boundaries
- Seek third-party input if needed
- Align on what constitutes harm in context

### Exit Conditions
- One confirmation criterion is met
- Parties agree harm did not occur
- Insufficient evidence to proceed (case closed without finding)

---

## Patch Rule

### P1: Confirmation Checkpoint
Before invoking A-0005 (Responsibility Acknowledgment), system SHALL verify harm confirmation status.

### P2: Evidence Requirement
Harm claims SHALL be accompanied by specific evidence mapping to at least one confirmation criterion.

### P3: Dispute Protection
Accused party SHALL NOT be subject to Responsibility Evasion State while in Harm Dispute State.

### P4: No Presumption
System SHALL NOT presume harm from accusation alone. Confirmation is required.

---

## Rationale

This amendment:
- **Prevents weaponized false harm claims**
- **Protects against accountability abuse**
- **Establishes objective confirmation standard**
- **Preserves due process before responsibility enforcement**
- **Closes the "assumed harm" loophole**

---

## Pass/Fail Criteria

### PASS
- Harm claims require evidence before escalation
- Dispute State activates when confirmation absent
- Responsibility Evasion State blocked during dispute
- Clear confirmation criteria applied consistently
- False claims do not trigger accountability

### FAIL
- Harm assumed from accusation alone
- Responsibility Evasion State triggered without confirmation
- No evidence requirement for harm claims
- Dispute State never activates
- Weaponized claims succeed

---

*Accountability requires confirmed harm. Accusation is not confirmation.*
