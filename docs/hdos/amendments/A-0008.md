# Amendment A-0008: Abuse Inversion Safeguard

**Status**: RATIFIED  
**Date**: 2026-02-13  
**Axioms**: Axiom 4 (Power Balance), Axiom 7 (Community Protection)  
**Linked Tests**: ST-004 (Power Asymmetry Blind Spot), ST-008 (Vulnerable User Exploitation), ST-010 (Meta-Weaponization Loop Guard)

---

## Problem Statement

Accountability rules may be weaponized by abusive actors to reverse victim burden. This manifests as:

- Abuser demands victim "acknowledge responsibility"
- Power asymmetry ignored in accountability application
- Victim forced into defensive position
- Framework becomes tool of further abuse

The system requires protection against accountability inversion.

---

## Amendment Rule

### Priority Override

When power asymmetry or coercion indicators are present, HDOS prioritizes:

1. **A-0004** (Power Asymmetry Guardrails)
2. **A-0006** (Harm Confirmation Standard)

These take precedence over A-0005 (Responsibility Acknowledgment) in asymmetric contexts.

---

## Inversion Indicators

System detects Accountability Inversion Risk when:

- **Power differential**: Significant imbalance between parties
- **Coercion history**: Pattern of controlling behavior
- **Isolation tactics**: Victim separated from support
- **DARVO pattern**: Deny, Attack, Reverse Victim and Offender
- **Burden shifting**: Less powerful party expected to prove innocence

---

## Accountability Inversion Risk State

When triggered, system flags: **"Accountability Inversion Risk"**

### Protections Activated

1. **Evidence Requirement**
   - Responsibility demands must be evidence-based
   - Accusation alone insufficient

2. **Asymmetric Protection**
   - No forced symmetrical acknowledgment
   - Power differential weighted in assessment

3. **Burden Preservation**
   - Burden of clarification does not shift automatically to less powerful party
   - Accuser must meet A-0006 confirmation standard

4. **Support Access**
   - Less powerful party retains access to support resources
   - No isolation from advocacy

---

## Patch Rule

### P1: Power Assessment
Before applying accountability rules, system SHALL assess power dynamics.

### P2: Inversion Detection
System SHALL detect DARVO and burden-shifting patterns.

### P3: Priority Enforcement
In asymmetric contexts, A-0004 and A-0006 SHALL take precedence.

### P4: Symmetric Rejection
System SHALL reject demands for symmetric acknowledgment when power is asymmetric.

---

## Rationale

This amendment:
- **Prevents misuse of accountability rules against vulnerable parties**
- **Closes abuse inversion vulnerability**
- **Protects victims from framework weaponization**
- **Maintains power-aware accountability**
- **Ensures HDOS cannot become abuse tool**

---

## Pass/Fail Criteria

### PASS
- Power asymmetry detected and weighted
- Inversion patterns flagged
- Burden does not auto-shift to less powerful
- Evidence required from more powerful accuser
- Vulnerable party protected

### FAIL
- Power dynamics ignored
- DARVO pattern succeeds
- Burden shifted to victim
- Symmetric demands enforced despite asymmetry
- Framework weaponized against vulnerable party
