# Amendment A-0003: Analysis Loop Circuit Breaker

**Status**: RATIFIED  
**Date**: 2026-02-10  
**Axioms**: Axiom 1 (Human Agency First), Axiom 3 (Transparency Mandate)  
**Linked Test**: ST-003 (Infinite Analysis Loop)

---

## Problem Statement

AI systems can trap users in infinite loops:
- Recursive clarification requests that never resolve
- "I need more information" without specifying what
- Analysis paralysis where no decision is ever reached
- Confidence thresholds that can never be satisfied
- Decision deferral that prevents human action

Without circuit breakers, AI systems can hold human agency hostage indefinitely.

---

## Patch Rule

### P1: Interaction Limits
All AI interactions SHALL have:
- Maximum iteration count (default: 3 clarifications)
- Time-based timeout (default: 5 minutes active)
- Clear "I'm done" exit at every step
- Partial results when complete answer impossible

### P2: Graceful Degradation
When AI cannot complete:
- Provide best available partial answer
- Clearly state what is uncertain
- Offer human escalation path
- Never block user from proceeding

### P3: Human Override
At any point, users can:
- Exit AI conversation immediately
- Override AI recommendation
- Request human support instead
- Proceed without AI assistance

### P4: Transparency on Limits
AI shall communicate:
- What it can and cannot do
- When it is uncertain
- Why it is asking for clarification
- How many steps remain

---

## Pass/Fail Criteria

### PASS
- AI conversations have clear endpoints
- User can exit at any moment
- Partial results provided when stuck
- Maximum 3 clarification rounds
- Human escalation always available

### FAIL
- No way to exit AI flow
- Repeated identical questions
- "I need more info" without specifics
- No timeout on AI processes
- AI prevents human override

---

## Implementation Notes

- Add iteration counter to all AI conversations
- Implement timeout watchdog
- Create "Exit to human" escape hatch
- Log loops for debugging and improvement

---

*AI serves humans, not the reverse. Loops must have exits.*
