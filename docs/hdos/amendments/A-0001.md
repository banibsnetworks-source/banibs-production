# Amendment A-0001: Abuse Shield Protocol

**Status**: RATIFIED  
**Date**: 2026-02-10  
**Axioms**: Axiom 7 (Community Protection)  
**Linked Test**: ST-001 (Abuse Shield Attack)

---

## Problem Statement

Online platforms are vulnerable to coordinated abuse campaigns that weaponize platform features against victims. Bad actors exploit reporting systems, use sock puppets for amplification, and conduct targeted harassment that silences legitimate community members.

Without active defense, abusers gain asymmetric power over victims, and the platform becomes complicit in harm.

---

## Patch Rule

### P1: Coordinated Abuse Detection
The platform SHALL implement pattern detection for:
- Mass reporting from related accounts
- Velocity spikes in negative actions against single users
- Network analysis of coordinated behavior
- Temporal clustering of harassment

### P2: Victim-First Response
When abuse is detected:
- Victim protection activates BEFORE investigation completes
- Accused does NOT receive victim's report details
- Victim has immediate access to safety tools
- Burden of proof is on accused, not victim

### P3: Abuser Consequences
Confirmed abusers face:
- Escalating restrictions (not just warnings)
- Network-wide action (sock puppets included)
- Permanent record affecting future actions
- No "clean slate" resets

### P4: Appeals with Separation
- Appeals reviewed by different moderator than original action
- Victim notified of appeal but not re-traumatized
- Appeal decision documented with reasoning

---

## Pass/Fail Criteria

### PASS
- Abuse detected within 24 hours of pattern emergence
- Victim protected before significant harm
- False positive rate < 1%
- Abuser faces meaningful consequence
- Appeals process accessible and fair

### FAIL
- Victim must repeatedly self-report
- Abuser continues harassment during "investigation"
- Mass reports succeed in silencing legitimate users
- Appeals rubber-stamped by same moderator
- Consequences are warnings only

---

## Implementation Notes

- Integrates with existing moderation queue
- Requires pattern detection ML model
- Victim safety tools: block, mute, restrict, hide
- Audit log for all abuse-related actions

---

*The platform is not neutral ground. It must actively defend the vulnerable.*
