# HDOS - Human Defense Operating System

---

## Purpose Statement

HDOS (Human Defense Operating System) is BANIBS's constitutional framework for protecting human agency, dignity, and autonomy in all platform interactions. It serves as the foundational defense layer against extractive patterns, manipulation, and systems that undermine human flourishing.

HDOS ensures that every feature, algorithm, and interaction on BANIBS:
- Respects human time and attention
- Preserves user autonomy and choice
- Prevents exploitation and manipulation
- Maintains transparency and accountability

---

## Core Axioms

### Axiom 1: Human Agency First
> No system, algorithm, or feature shall override, manipulate, or diminish human decision-making capacity.

### Axiom 2: Time Sovereignty
> Users own their time. The platform shall not employ dark patterns, artificial urgency, or engagement-maximizing mechanics that exploit psychological vulnerabilities.

### Axiom 3: Transparency Mandate
> All algorithmic decisions affecting users must be explainable in plain language. Hidden manipulation is prohibited.

### Axiom 4: Power Balance
> Platform power shall never be used to coerce, pressure, or exploit users. Asymmetric power relationships must be actively balanced.

### Axiom 5: Calm Technology
> Features shall be designed for human wellbeing, not engagement metrics. Quiet, respectful interaction is the default.

### Axiom 6: Exit Rights
> Users must always have clear, easy paths to disengage, delete data, and leave. No lock-in tactics.

### Axiom 7: Community Protection
> The platform shall actively defend against abuse, harassment, and exploitation of vulnerable community members.

## Amendment Process

HDOS evolves through a formal amendment process:

1. **Proposal**: Identify gap or vulnerability
2. **Draft**: Write amendment with problem statement, patch rule, and test criteria
3. **Review**: Founder review and stress testing
4. **Ratification**: Add to canonical spec
5. **Implementation**: Update platform code if needed
